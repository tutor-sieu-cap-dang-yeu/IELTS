Oil Production Capacity

Bar chart · 6 nước vùng Vịnh × 4 năm (1990–2010) · luyện viết 5 bước rồi nộp để chấm
📊 Bar chart xu hướng 🪜 Scaffolding 5 bước 🎯 Mục tiêu Band 7.5 · 4 tiêu chí
Đề bài: The bar chart below shows the estimated oil production capacity of six Gulf countries from 1990 to 2010. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. (Viết tối thiểu 150 từ · khoảng 20 phút.)
Đơn vị: triệu thùng/ngày (millions of barrels per day). 4 cột mỗi nước = 4 năm: 1990 · 2000 · 2005 · 2010.

5WRITING THE BODY — Viết thân bàisố liệu + so sánh
Mục tiêu: dịch các ý tiếng Việt thành 2 đoạn thân bài, có số liệu và so sánh.
TAGRABody 1 — Saudi Arabia tương phản Qatar (cao nhất ↔ thấp nhất)
  • Saudi Arabia: 1990 sản xuất hơn 8 triệu thùng/ngày, tăng đều và đạt đỉnh hơn 14 triệu vào 2010.
  • Trái lại, Qatar là nước sản xuất ít nhất.
  • Dù tăng nhẹ vào năm 2000, Qatar giữ tương đối ổn định ở mức dưới 1 triệu thùng/ngày.
⇨ Write Body 1 here …
GỢI Ý BODY 1 From 1990 to 2010, there was a significant increase in oil production in Saudi Arabia. In 1990, just over 8 million barrels per day were produced and this rose steadily, reaching a peak of over 14 million barrels per day in 2010. In contrast, the smallest producer was Qatar. Despite a slight increase in 2000, production in Qatar remained relatively stable at less than 1 million barrels per day.
💡 "just over 8" / "over 14" / "less than 1" — với bar chart không ghi số chính xác, dùng ngôn ngữ xấp xỉ là an toàn và đúng chuẩn.
TACCBody 2 — 4 nước còn lại (Iran · Iraq · Kuwait · UAE)
  • Cả 4 nước đều tăng nhẹ trong giai đoạn.
  • Iran năm 1990 đã hơn 3 triệu thùng/ngày — cao hơn 3 nước kia (quanh 2 triệu).
  • Riêng UAE không tăng vào năm 2000. Đến 2010, Iran đạt đỉnh hơn 4 triệu; Iraq, Kuwait, UAE đạt gần 4 triệu.
⇨ Write Body 2 here …
GỢI Ý BODY 2 The other countries saw a slight rise in oil production over the period. The estimated figure for Iran in 1990 was over 3 million barrels per day, compared with around 2 million barrels in the other three countries. Although production did not increase in 2000 in the UAE, the amount of oil produced peaked in 2010 in Iran at over 4 million barrels and at almost 4 million barrels in Iraq, Kuwait and the UAE.

MODEL ANSWER · BAND 7.5 The bar chart illustrates the estimated amount of oil produced in 6 countries between 1990 and 2010, measured in millions of barrels per day.

Overall, oil production capacity tended to increase during this 20-year period. The figures for Saudi Arabia were significantly higher than in the other countries, while production was lowest in Qatar.

From 1990 to 2010, there was a significant increase in oil production in Saudi Arabia. In 1990, just over 8 million barrels per day were produced and this rose steadily, reaching a peak of over 14 million barrels per day in 2010. In contrast, the smallest producer was Qatar. Despite a slight increase in 2000, production in Qatar remained relatively stable at less than 1 million barrels per day.

The other countries saw a slight rise in oil production over the period. The estimated figure for Iran in 1990 was over 3 million barrels per day, compared with around 2 million barrels in the other three countries. Although production did not increase in 2000 in the UAE, the amount of oil produced peaked in 2010 in Iran at over 4 million barrels and at almost 4 million barrels in Iraq, Kuwait and the UAE.
✅ Vì sao đạt 7.5: TA — overview nêu 2 đặc điểm (xu hướng chung + cao/thấp nhất), có so sánh xuyên suốt. CC — chia đoạn theo plan rõ, dùng "In contrast / compared with / Although". LR — "produce/production" (không "manufacture"), "rose steadily / reached a peak / remained relatively stable". GRA — bị động ("were produced"), participle clause ("reaching a peak…"), mệnh đề nhượng bộ ("Although / Despite"). Past simple nhất quán.
