Fast Food Expenditure

Đề bài: The bar chart shows the amount of money spent on three types of fast food by three income groups in the UK in 1990. Write a report for a university lecturer describing the information. (≥150 từ · ~20 phút.)
Đơn vị: pence/tuần. High income: burger 42, fish&chips 17, pizza 19 · Average: 32/25/12 · Low: 14/17/7.

5WRITING THE BODY — Viết thân bàisố liệu + so sánh
Mục tiêu: dịch các ý tiếng Việt thành 2 đoạn, có số liệu và so sánh.
TAGRABody 1 — nhóm High & Average income
  • Nhóm high chi nhiều nhất: ~42 pence/tuần cho hamburger; 17 và 19 pence cho fish&chips và pizza (lần lượt).
  • Nhóm average cũng chi cho burger nhiều nhất: 33 pence; fish&chips theo sát ở 25 pence.
  • Khoản chi thấp nhất của nhóm average là 12 pence cho pizza.
⇨ Write Body 1 here …
GỢI Ý BODY 1People in the high income group spent the most money. They spent about 42 pence per week on hamburgers, and 17 and 19 pence per week on fish and chips and pizza respectively. In the average income group, consumers also spent more on hamburgers than on the other fast foods. Their spending on burgers was 33 pence per week, although fish and chips came a close second at 25 pence. The lowest expenditure was just 12 pence on pizza.

  • Nhưng tổng thể chi rất ít: chỉ 17 pence cho fish&chips, 14 pence cho hamburger, 7 pence cho pizza.

GỢI Ý BODY 2People spent more on fish and chips than on burgers and pizza in the low income group. However, spending was relatively low, at just 17 pence per week on fish and chips, 14 pence on hamburgers and only 7 pence on pizza.

MODEL ANSWER · BAND 7.5The bar chart compares expenditure by three income groups on three types of fast food in the UK in 1990.

Overall, while all three income groups bought fast foods, there were differences in the type of fast food eaten by each group. It is also clear that the low income group spent the lowest amount.

People in the high income group spent the most money. They spent about 42 pence per week on hamburgers, and 17 and 19 pence per week on fish and chips and pizza respectively. In the average income group, consumers also spent more on hamburgers than on the other fast foods. Their spending on burgers was 33 pence per week, although fish and chips came a close second at 25 pence. The lowest expenditure was just 12 pence on pizza.

People spent more on fish and chips than on burgers and pizza in the low income group. However, spending was relatively low, at just 17 pence per week on fish and chips, 14 pence on hamburgers and only 7 pence on pizza.
✅ Vì sao đạt 7.5: TA — overview nêu khác biệt loại đồ ăn + nhóm thấp chi ít nhất, so sánh xuyên suốt. CC — "while / although / However / respectively". LR — "came a close second, expenditure, relatively low". GRA — bị động ngầm, mệnh đề nhượng bộ (although), so sánh (more…than). ⚠️ Đây là biểu đồ 1 năm nên KHÔNG có ngôn ngữ tăng/giảm — đúng luật comparative.
