Describe an Object

Plan and deliver a 2-minute talk about any object — a gadget, a piece of clothing, a painting, a film or even a plant — with a clear structure and precise, natural vocabulary.

🎯 By the end of this lesson, you can…
  • recognise the 3 object groups: objects, contents and living things
  • organise the talk: Intro → Context → Main content → Conclusion
  • open directly, or with an indirect lead-in
  • tell how you got or found an object and give a vivid first impression
  • develop the main content by listing features or by storytelling
  • praise or criticise an object with natural, idiomatic frames
  • describe design, size, shape, colour, material, age, price and quality precisely
